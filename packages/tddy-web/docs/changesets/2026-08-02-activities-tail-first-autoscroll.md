**Type:** Feature

The frame→entry projection moved out of the stream effect into **`acpReplayProjection.ts`** so a whole page can be projected at once, with keys scoped by the page's absolute `firstSeq` — two pages share one rendered list after a prepend and React reconciles by key, so a collision silently drops or reuses a row. `agentActivityRegistry` holds the loaded range as `olderMessages` + the fed range (the feed rewrites its own entries wholesale on every frame, which would otherwise drop everything paged in behind them), plus `oldestSeq`/`atOldest`/`loadingOlder`, and **ignores a page whose `firstSeq` is not strictly below the cursor** — that arithmetic is the only thing standing between a duplicate in-flight response and a doubled transcript, which is why the cursor is an absolute position rather than an opaque token. New pure **`scrollFollow.ts`** (`isPinnedToBottom` 32 px, `isNearTop` 64 px, `scrollTopAfterPrepend`) keeps the viewport decisions testable without a rendered container. The transcript owns sticky-bottom follow in a `useLayoutEffect` (so neither the follow scroll nor the prepend compensation is ever a visible jump), a jump-to-latest affordance counting *entries* since detach via a key anchor — so a coalesced tool-call refinement and a prepended page both leave the count alone — and a near-top page trigger fired from the **gesture** rather than a render, which is what makes a failed page retryable without being retried unprompted. `overflowAnchor: "none"` on the scroll container, or the browser's own scroll anchoring applies a second, uncoordinated correction on top of the compensating one; the indicator and the affordance render **outside** the container, since content whose height appears and disappears around a prepend would corrupt the measurement the compensation depends on. **The scroll container's flex/overflow is declared inline as well as in Tailwind, all the way down the ancestor chain** — the component harness loads no stylesheet, so a class-only declaration leaves it unscrollable and every follow/paging test vacuous; a bounded height that stops one level above the container bounds nothing. The transcript is keyed by `sessionId` at both call sites: neither surface remounts on a switch, so a reader scrolled up in one session would otherwise land in the next still detached, compensate a phantom prepend against the previous transcript's height, and — since keys are position-scoped and two sessions can share them — see the other session's jump-to-latest count. `AgentChat.tsx` split 569 → 99 as a facade over `LiveAgentChatView` (235) and `AgentTranscriptView` (152) with shared `chatEntryPresentation` (41) and `useTailFollow` (169); all four public exports kept their name and path, so none of the ~100 specs importing `AgentChatView` changed.
