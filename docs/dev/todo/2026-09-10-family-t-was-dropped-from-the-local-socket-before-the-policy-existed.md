# 2026-09-10 — Family T was dropped from the local socket, before the policy that forbids it existed

**Category:** Defect
**Source:** `#unbundle` node 7, [#476](https://github.com/uppin/tddy-coder/pull/476) — discovered
while setting the local-socket reachability policy

`connection.ConnectionService` carried **all 90 methods** on the daemon's local Unix socket, so any
local caller could reach any family. `tddy-sandbox-app/src/daemon_client.rs` dials that socket today,
and nothing constrains callers outside this repo.

The `#unbundle` stack has been deciding, per node and without recording it, whether a family that
leaves stays reachable there:

| Node | Families | Local socket |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ([#470](https://github.com/uppin/tddy-coder/pull/470)) | E, F, G, H | **preserved** — `host_tonic_adapter.rs` (8 methods), `worktree_tonic_adapter.rs` (9), both `add_service`d |
| 4 ([#473](https://github.com/uppin/tddy-coder/pull/473)) | T | **dropped** — no `livekit_tonic_adapter.rs`, no `tonic_livekit` module in `tddy-service`, and `local_socket_server.rs` still serves three services |

Node 1 was conservative and wrote a test (`local_token_uds.rs`) that locks its choice in. Node 4 did
the opposite. **Neither recorded a decision**, and the result is that `StreamLiveKitRooms` was
reachable over the local socket before the stack and is not after it.

## The policy, set in node 7

> Every family that was reachable on the local Unix socket as part of `connection.ConnectionService`
> stays reachable there after it moves.

The reasoning: dropping a family is a **silent capability removal on a privileged local interface**,
and its failure mode is a caller that used to work receiving `unimplemented` with no announcement.
Nodes 7 and 8 follow it (17 and 16 adapter methods, generated by node 6's `generate_tonic_adapter`),
and node 9 follows it for the two methods `tddy-sandbox-app` genuinely dials.

## Why this is an entry rather than a fix

Family T's gap is in **node 4's** files — `local_socket_server.rs` and a `livekit_tonic_adapter.rs`
that does not exist. Node 4 is a predecessor of 6, 7, 8 and 9, and is already greened; editing it
from a descendant is what the `pr-stack` model's forward-only rule forbids, and rewriting it would
cascade-stale four nodes.

## The fix

Once node 6's generator exists, family T's adapter is a generated file plus one `add_service` line —
the cheapest possible use of it, and a useful first exercise on a service that is not node 6's own.
Do it either as a commit on node 4's branch while it is still open, or as a follow-up PR after the
stack lands.

Also worth revisiting at the same time: node 1's `local_token_uds.rs` asserts `HostServiceClient` and
`WorktreeServiceClient` over the socket. Under the policy that is now correct rather than
over-conservative — but it was written before the policy existed, so nothing recorded *why* those two
clients are exercised there.
