# 2026-11-17 — An idle orchestrator is woken by what it watches

An orchestrator with live session watches that is idle no longer waits for a human or its next
tool call: the daemon sends it one turn — a pasted `[tddy watch]` message listing what its
watches matched, each event with its session, kind and `request_id`, plus anything lost and the
watches that ended. Idle means a live main terminal, no pending question of its own, and a
reported status of `Done`, `WaitingForInput`, `Started` or `Ended`; a busy orchestrator is woken
once its turn ends with events still waiting. Bursts coalesce over a 2-second settle and wakes of
one orchestrator are at least 30 seconds apart. The events in a wake count as delivered and do
not ride on a later tool answer; a failed paste delivers nothing. A project-home main session
holding a live watch is not auto-stopped while it waits. See
[Project concept § Watching sessions](../project-concept.md#watching-sessions) and
[Project-home auto-stop](../project-home-auto-stop.md).
